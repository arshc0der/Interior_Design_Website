# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a friendly, safe and welcoming place for everyone, whatever their experience level, age, gender, background, ability, religion, nationality or appearance. Beginners are especially welcome.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient and respectful.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept feedback gracefully. Reviews help everyone learn.
- Help newcomers. Everyone was a beginner once.
- Assume good intentions and ask questions when something is unclear.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination
- Personal attacks or trolling
- Sexual or violent language or images
- Publishing someone's private information
- Spam, self-promotion unrelated to the project
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and comments.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, or block people who break these rules.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [@arshc0der](https://github.com/arshc0der), through GitHub. Reports are handled privately and fairly.
